# ProZ0 Code of Conduct

ProZ0 expects professional, respectful collaboration across companies, disciplines and community contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Critique work, evidence and decisions—not people.
- Communicate clearly and in good faith.
- Respect role boundaries and recorded project decisions.
- Give actionable review feedback.
- Record important project conclusions where teammates can find them.
- Welcome disagreement when it improves the product or reveals risk.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality and security boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, personal attacks, doxxing, sexual harassment, deliberate sabotage, credential exposure, or repeated bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

## Project discussions

Technical, design and product disagreement should be resolved through evidence, role authority and the Source of Truth—not seniority theatre, company identity, or personal pressure.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may hide/remove abusive content, lock discussions, restrict participation, or escalate severe conduct issues to the Project Owner and relevant organization.

Security-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md` rather than this conduct process.
