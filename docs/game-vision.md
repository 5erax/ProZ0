# ProZ0 game vision

## High concept

After a planetary catastrophe leaves only a fraction of humanity, a small group reaches a new planet. They begin with wreckage, limited supplies, and no infrastructure. Together they must survive, establish a permanent settlement, rebuild human capability, and learn why the planet contains traces of a vanished civilization.

## Player fantasy

The player should feel like a true pioneer: every safe room, mapped route, machine, crop, and recovered ruin exists because the group made it happen. The world should create stories through preparation, risk, mistakes, rescue expeditions, and visible colony growth.

## North-star goals

1. Rebuild a self-sustaining human colony.
2. Explore far enough to understand the planet's previous civilization.
3. Let players choose their own order, specialization, base location, and pace.

## Tone and boundaries

The tone is hopeful, lonely, curious, and occasionally tense. Survival is balanced rather than relentlessly punitive. Combat protects exploration and the colony; it is not the primary identity. There is no permanent class lock and no mandatory raid schedule.

## Narrative layers

- **Humanity story:** establish shelter, food, power, industry, medicine, logistics, and eventually a settlement capable of receiving more survivors.
- **Planet mystery:** strange materials lead to structures, ruins, records, and the question of what happened to the earlier civilization.

## Design constraints

The world is persistent and mutable, so every generated chunk must be saveable. The MVP must prove the core fantasy before advanced automation, NPC colonies, aircraft, or multi-planet travel are attempted.
