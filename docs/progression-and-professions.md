# Progression, professions, and research

## Three layers

### Character level
All meaningful activities grant XP, weighted by difficulty and complexity: gathering, crafting, building, discovering, researching, repairing, and combat. Simple repetitive actions should not dominate progression.

### Personal skill tree
Every pioneer begins with basic survival: gathering, carrying, crafting, first aid, navigation, endurance, and basic combat. Skill points improve the individual.

### Profession

Profession unlock requires level, prerequisite skills, and a profession quest. A player can unlock every profession over time; ordinary players naturally specialize in one or two.

Initial professions:

- **Explorer:** navigation, scouting, mobility, survival.
- **Engineer:** machines, electricity, repair.
- **Scientist:** research and alien technology.
- **Botanist:** farming, biology, medicine.
- **Miner:** geology, extraction, mining safety.
- **Builder:** structures, architecture, habitat efficiency.
- **Hunter:** wildlife, combat, tracking.

## Colony research

Research belongs to the world/colony, while skills belong to the player. A skilled Engineer still needs the colony to research a reactor, and a newcomer can use colony technology while developing their own skills. Research consumes discoveries, resources, facilities, and time.

## Level target

The first prototype should take a new player toward level 3–4 and demonstrate the path from survival basics to the first meaningful specialization choice.
