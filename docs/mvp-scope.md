# MVP scope

## The proof to build

The MVP is a 30–60 minute vertical slice that answers one question: does it feel compelling to land on an unknown planet with friends, prepare for risk, build a first home, and discover evidence of a prior civilization?

## Included

- Desktop browser target with 2D pixel top-down/3/4 movement.
- One deterministic generated region with expandable chunk boundaries.
- Solo and small hosted co-op foundation; target 2–4 players first, with architecture for 10.
- Fog of war and shared discovery.
- Basic terrain, trees/plants, ore, water, food, wildlife, one hostile encounter, and one ruin.
- Weight-based player inventory, basic containers, death drop, respawn, XP/durability loss.
- Gathering, crafting, repair, basic health/temperature/water needs.
- Landing module, habitat room, corridor, storage, workbench, power unit, and first machine.
- Basic day/night, one weather event, and a readable expedition loop.
- First level progression and one or two profession quests as a prototype.

## Deferred

Full procedural planet scale, many biomes, advanced automation, NPC colonists, aircraft, deep farming genetics, PvP, multi-planet travel, complex alien factions, and a complete endgame civilization simulation.

## Acceptance criteria

After one session, a player can explain where they landed, what they discovered, what they built, what danger stopped them, and what they want to investigate next. Two players can share a map discovery and recover a dropped inventory together.
