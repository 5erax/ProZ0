# Gameplay pillars and loops

## Four pillars

### Exploration
Reveal the planet by travelling through it. Discover resources, biomes, wildlife, hazards, ruins, technology, and future colony sites. Distance increases both reward and risk.

### Balanced survival
Food, water, medicine, tools, clothing, weapons, transport, and inventory space matter on expeditions. A mistake can ruin a trip without erasing the entire world.

### Creative colony building
Build a practical home that visibly grows from a landing module into a connected settlement and industrial base.

### Consequence-driven ecology
Mining, pollution, noise, light, wildlife hunting, and territorial expansion change local behaviour and can trigger events.

## Loops

- **5–15 minutes:** explore nearby → gather → manage needs → return → craft or repair.
- **30–120 minutes:** prepare expedition → enter a new biome → manage hazards and capacity → recover resources or lore → return or risk pushing farther.
- **Long term:** survive → establish base → specialize → research → industrialize → automate → expand → study the ruins → rebuild civilization.

## Success criteria

The first session should make players want to reopen the same world. They should have a meaningful base, a shared discovery, a reason to prepare better, and an unanswered question beyond the visible frontier.
