# Survival, exploration, and ecology

## Needs and preparation

The MVP should focus on food, water, health, stamina, temperature exposure, equipment condition, and carrying capacity. Later hazards can add oxygen, radiation, illness, and specialist environmental protection.

## Death and recovery

Death respawns the player at the base. Their carried inventory remains at the death site for a recovery expedition. The player loses a small amount of XP and equipment durability. Recovery should be tense but achievable by the team.

## Inventory

Use a weight-and-container model. Items have weight, volume, stack rules, condition, and category. Backpacks, rover cargo, storage modules, and later warehouses are nested containers. Capacity is a meaningful expedition decision.

## Resources

Plants, wildlife, and wood are renewable; some minerals and crystals recover slowly; special local deposits can be finite; deep mining, geothermal extraction, and atmospheric harvesting can provide late-game supply. Respawn is gradual and resource-specific.

## Ecology and events

Track planetary pressure from mining, pollution, noise, light, expansion, and hunting. Pressure changes wildlife behaviour, alien nest activity, predator movement, and event likelihood. Events are caused by player actions or environment, not a mandatory calendar raid.

## Time and weather

Night is colder, darker, and more dangerous. Weather includes rain, sandstorms, acid rain, meteor storms, and solar flares. Weather must affect decisions: visibility, temperature, power stability, travel, crop health, or machine reliability.
