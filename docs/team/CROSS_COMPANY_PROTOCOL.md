# Cross-company collaboration

**Version:** 2.0.0. Both companies share one backlog and product direction; authority follows the role, not company.

PM-A and PM-B maintain shared visibility of active/blocked tasks, capacity, locks, PRs, reviews, handoffs and integration risk. One Coordinating PM owns each task; the other may inspect, discuss and flag a collision. Changes to the other's task require recorded agreement or predesignated backup coverage.

## Direct collaboration

Specialists discuss interfaces, constraints, findings and risk directly on the source Issue/PR when permitted. A preauthorized review request goes to its named reviewer without needing both PMs to relay it. Neither company is an approval gate merely because a reviewer belongs to it.

Technical decisions go to TL; mechanics to GD; canon to NWD; visual/UX standards to ART; validation verdicts to QA. Ordinary approved audio/spatial/content/tool craft remains with its specialist. Priority, assignment, locks and integration sequencing belong to PMs. PO resolves product/major scope/governance decisions, not routine factual clarification.

## Change request

Record task, requesting/current PM, exact proposed ownership/scope/priority change, rationale, impact, blocked portion and response deadline. Continue non-overlapping authorized work. Do not silently close, release or rescope the other PM's task.

## Availability and dispatch

For a blocking question identify recipient, endpoint, response-needed-by and backup. Use current verified identity/runtime routing; a role label in a comment may be only POSTED, not DELIVERED or ACKNOWLEDGED. A PM cycle should reconcile orphan tasks and unacknowledged handoffs. A documented queue is not an installed scheduler.

Both PMs use the same WIP/capacity policy. If a shared specialist is overloaded, prioritize the common critical path and review queue, rather than accumulating separate company roadmaps. Coverage must be recorded before a backup changes state; absence is not consent.
